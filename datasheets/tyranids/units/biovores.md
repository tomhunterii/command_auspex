## Abilities

### Core
**Deadly Demise 1.** When this model is destroyed, roll one D6 before removing it from play. On a 6, each unit within 6" of that model suffers 1 mortal wound.

### Faction
**Synapse.** While a Tyranids unit from your army is within Synapse Range of your army: each time that unit takes a Battle-shock test, take that test on 3D6 instead of 2D6; and each time a model in that unit makes a melee attack, add 1 to the Strength characteristic of that attack.

> Note: Biovores carry the **Synapse** keyword on the current Wahapedia datasheet — i.e. they project Synapse to nearby Tyranids units and benefit from the Codex army-rule. Confirm against codex print.

**Shadow in the Warp.** Once per battle, in either player's Command phase, if one or more units from your army with this ability are on the battlefield, you can unleash the Shadow in the Warp. When you do, each enemy unit on the battlefield must take a Battle-shock test. Each time an enemy unit takes such a test, if it is within 6" of one or more Synapse units from your army, subtract 1 from that test.

### Seed Spore Mines
Once per turn, in your Shooting phase, when selected to shoot, one unit with this ability can use it instead of making any attacks with its ranged weapons. If it does, you can add one new SPORE MINES unit to your army and set it up anywhere on the battlefield that is wholly within 48" of this unit and more than 9" horizontally away from all enemy units. That SPORE MINES unit contains 1 model for each model in this unit.

## Tactical Notes

- **48" [INDIRECT FIRE]** with [BLAST] and [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] — drop them out of LoS behind terrain and lob D3 S6 AP-1 D2 shots at infantry blobs. [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] turns Critical Wounds into mortal wounds.
- **Seed Spore Mines** is the alternative to shooting: use it on turns where there are no priority targets, or to clog deep-strike lanes.
- T6 W5 Sv3+ is solid for a back-line unit; protect from Deep Strike alpha-strikes and snipers.
