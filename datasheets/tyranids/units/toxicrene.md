## Abilities

### Core
**Deadly Demise D3.** When this model is destroyed, roll one D6 before removing it from play. On a 6, each unit within 6" of that model suffers D3 mortal wounds.

### Grasping Tendrils
Each time an enemy unit (excluding TITANIC units) within Engagement Range of this model is selected to Fall Back, that unit's controlling player must take a test by rolling one D6: on a 1 or 2, that unit cannot Fall Back this phase and remains stationary instead.

### Hyper-toxic Miasma (Aura)
At the end of your Movement phase, roll one D6 for each enemy unit within 6" of this model:
- on a 2 or 3, that unit suffers 1 mortal wound;
- on a 4 or 5, that unit suffers D3 mortal wounds;
- on a 6, that unit suffers D6 mortal wounds.

## Tactical Notes

- **Massive toxic lashes** has both a ranged AND melee profile using the same weapon name (9"/Melee). [ANTI-INFANTRY 2+] means Critical Wounds on a 2+ to wound vs INFANTRY targets — devastating into MEQ/GEQ blobs.
- **2D6 ranged attacks at 9"** average ~7 shots. Pair with the 12 melee attacks for an ~19-attack profile in close combat after a charge.
- **Hyper-toxic Miasma** is end-of-Movement, so position aggressively — wrap an enemy infantry unit to land the aura, and the Grasping Tendrils sticky-engagement keeps them there.
- **No invul, T11 W14 Sv3+** — extremely durable into S5/S6 small-arms (wounding on 5s/6s). Vulnerable to S10+ AP-2 weapons; screen with chaff or attach a Maleceptor / Norn Emissary aura.
