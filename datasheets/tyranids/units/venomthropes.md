## Abilities

### Core
**Stealth.** Each time a ranged attack targets this unit, if the attacking model is more than 12" away, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.

### Faction
**Synapse.** If your Army Faction is Tyranids, while a Tyranids unit from your army is within 6" of one or more friendly Synapse models, that Tyranids unit is said to be within Synapse Range of that model and of your army.

> **Note on Synapse:** The Wahapedia extract lists Synapse under Faction. Venomthropes do NOT have the SYNAPSE keyword on their unit keyword list, so they do not act as a Synapse source themselves. They benefit from Synapse like any other Tyranids unit. Treat Synapse on this datasheet as reference text for the army-wide rule.

### Foul Spores (Aura)
While a friendly Tyranids unit is within 6" of this unit, each time a ranged attack targets that unit, models in that unit have the Benefit of Cover against that attack. In addition, while a friendly Tyranids unit (excluding Monsters) is within 6" of this unit, models in that unit have the Stealth ability.

## Tactical Notes

- **Aura is called Foul Spores**, not Shrouding Spores. This matters when looking up interactions with Stratagems or enemy rules that name the aura.
- The aura grants **Benefit of Cover** (not "-1 to hit ranged"). Models already in cover get no stacking armour save improvement on infantry (cover caps), but they do benefit from the +1 Sv vs AP0 hits against Infantry, and Benefit of Cover allows some Stratagems/rules to trigger.
- Non-Monster Tyranids within 6" additionally gain **Stealth** (−1 to hit from ranged attackers more than 12" away).
- Monsters still get Benefit of Cover from the aura, but NOT Stealth.
- Venomthropes themselves have Stealth (Core).
