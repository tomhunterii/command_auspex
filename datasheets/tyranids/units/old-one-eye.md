## Abilities

### Core
- Feel No Pain 5+
- Leader

### Faction
**Synapse.** While a Tyranids unit from your army is within Synapse Range of your army: each time that unit takes a Battle-shock test, take that test on 3D6 instead of 2D6; and each time a model in that unit makes a melee attack, add 1 to the Strength characteristic of that attack.

> *(Old One Eye does NOT have the Synapse keyword in its unit keyword line per Wahapedia — it has the Synapse ability text listed, likely reflecting the bonded-Carnifexes effect. Verify in the physical codex if running competitive. For roster purposes, treat Old One Eye's attached bodyguard unit as benefiting from nearby Synapse models in the army, not Old One Eye itself.)*

### Alpha Leader
While this model is leading a unit, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, you can re-roll the Hit roll.

### Unstoppable Monster
At the start of each player's Command phase, this model regains up to D3 lost wounds.

> **KEY TACTICAL NOTES:**
> - **Regen (Unstoppable Monster):** D3 wounds healed at the start of **each player's Command phase** — i.e. potentially 2D3 per battle round (own turn + opponent's turn). Average ~4 W/round regen. Cannot exceed starting 9 W.
> - **Alpha Leader:** The re-roll Hit roll applies to every model in the attached Carnifexes unit, not just Old One Eye. Massive damage multiplier on a 3-Carnifex brick.
> - **No invuln:** Vulnerable to high-AP anti-tank. 2+ save is good but AP-3/-4 hits reduce to 5+/6+. Screen him or use cover.
