## Abilities

### Core
**Deep Strike.** (Per Wahapedia datasheet — verify against codex print before a tournament.)

### Faction
**Shadow in the Warp.** Each time an enemy unit is required to take a Battle-shock or Leadership test while it is within 12" of one or more units from your army with this ability, subtract 1 from that test.

**Synapse.** If your Army Faction is Tyranids, while a Tyranids unit from your army is within 6" of one or more friendly Synapse models, that Tyranids unit is said to be within Synapse Range of that model and of your army.

### Spirit Leech (Aura, Psychic)
While an enemy unit is within 6" of this unit, if this unit contains a Neurothrope, each time that enemy unit fails a Battle-shock test, it suffers D3 mortal wounds and one model in this unit regains up to D3 lost wounds.

### Warp Field (Aura, Psychic)
While a friendly Tyranids unit is within 6" of this unit, models in that unit have a 6+ invulnerable save.

> **Note:** Warp Field grants a **6+ invulnerable** to friendly Tyranids within 6" — it does NOT improve an existing better invulnerable. The Zoanthropes brood itself has a 4+ invulnerable printed in its own profile, independent of this aura.

## Tactical Notes

- **Warp Field is a 6+ invul aura** — it only helps Tyranids units that have no invul of their own or a worse than 6+ invul (i.e. basically any model without an invul). It does nothing for the Zoanthropes themselves (they already have 4+) or for Hive Tyrants / other models with a better printed invul.
- The witchfire Warp Blast profile is a BLAST D3 shots S7 AP-2 DD3 — the primary volume-damage option.
- The focused Warp Blast profile is S12 AP-3 DD6+1 with [LETHAL HITS] — designed to crack Monsters and heavy armour.
- Spirit Leech requires a Neurothrope in the unit and a failed Battle-shock by the target. Stacks well with Shadow in the Warp's -1 to Battle-shock within 12".
- Unit has FLY and Deep Strike (per Wahapedia) — pressure home objectives from Reserves.
