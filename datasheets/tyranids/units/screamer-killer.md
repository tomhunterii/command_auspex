## Abilities

### Core
**Deadly Demise 1.** When this model is destroyed, roll one D6 before removing it from play. On a 6, each unit within 6" of that model suffers 1 mortal wound.

### Death Scream
In your Shooting phase, after this model has shot, select one unit hit by one or more of those attacks. That unit must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from that test.

## Tactical Notes

- **Bio-plasmic scream** is [ASSAULT] [BLAST]: D6+3 shots S8 AP-2 D1 at 18", and you can fire it after Advancing. Hunt MEQ infantry blobs with it.
- **Screamer-killer talons** are 10 attacks at S10 AP-2 D3 — top-tier monster melee. S10 wounds T5 on 2s, T9 on 3s, T10 on 4s.
- **Death Scream** chains off any successful shot — pick the unit you most want to fail Battle-shock; -1 to that test, plus Shadow in the Warp's -1 if within 6" of a Synapse unit, plus Synapse forces 3D6 already if they're in your army's Synapse range (on enemy units it's a flat 2D6 with -1/-1 modifiers).
- **No degrading profile, no invul, T9 W10 Sv2+** — durable up front, but a single AP-3 D6 weapon will start carving wounds. Synaptic Imperative coverage and screening matter.
