## Abilities

### Core
None printed on datasheet.

### Faction
**Synapse.** If your Army Faction is Tyranids, while a Tyranids unit from your army is within 6" of one or more friendly Synapse models, that Tyranids unit is said to be within Synapse Range of that model and of your army.

### Endless Multitude
While this unit is within Synapse range of your army, each time one or more models in this unit are destroyed, roll one D6: on a 4+, one destroyed model is returned to this unit with its full wounds remaining. (Wording per Wahapedia datasheet; verify exact text in the codex before a tournament.)

> **Note:** Wahapedia occasionally summarises Endless Multitude. The effect on the 10th Ed datasheet is a Synapse-gated model-return mechanic. Confirm printed wording from the codex when possible.

### Skulking Horrors
Once per turn, when an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move within 9" of this unit, if this unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, it can make a Normal move of up to D6".

## Tactical Notes

- **Fleshborer is [ASSAULT], not [PISTOL].** You can Advance and still shoot it, but you cannot fire it while within Engagement Range of an enemy (that is the Pistol rule, which Fleshborer lacks). 18" range, S5 AP0 D1.
- **Spinefists** are the only Termagant ranged option with [PISTOL] — 12", A2, S3, [ASSAULT][PISTOL][TWIN-LINKED].
- **Strangleweb** auto-hits (Torrent), at S2 with [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] — fishes for 6s to wound to deal mortal wounds.
- **Endless Multitude** requires Synapse range to function; keep a Tervigon, Warriors, Broodlord, Neurothrope or similar nearby.
