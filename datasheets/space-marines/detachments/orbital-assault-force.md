# Orbital Assault Force (500 Worlds Titus Campaign)

**Faction:** Space Marines (Adeptus Astartes)
**Source:** 500 Worlds: Titus — Detachments booklet, pp. 4-5 (Warhammer Design Studio, 2026)
**Also see:** `500 Worlds Campaign/campaign/10-500worlds-detachments.md` (full transcription)

> **Campaign-specific detachment.** This is the 500 Worlds Titus campaign version, NOT the base Codex: Space Marines Orbital Assault Force (which differs in detachment-rule wording). Use this file for campaign games.

---

## Detachment Rule: Rapid-drop Deployment

At the start of the Declare Battle Formations step, select a number of **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** units (excluding **TITANIC** units) from your army based on the battle size:

| Battle Size | Number of Units |
|---|---|
| Incursion | 2 |
| **Strike Force** | **3** |
| Onslaught | 4 |

Models in those units have the **Deep Strike** ability.

Each time an **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** model from your army makes an attack:
- If its unit was **set up on the battlefield this turn**, re-roll a **Wound roll of 1**.
- If that unit **disembarked from a DROP POD** this turn, re-roll a **Hit roll of 1** as well.

> **Important:** This rule GRANTS the Deep Strike ability. It does NOT override the standard mission rule that Reserves cannot arrive in battle round 1 (see `project_40k_reserves_turn_1.md`). DS arrivals earliest in turn 2.

## Enhancements

**Points costs confirmed by Captain Hunter 2026-04-21.** The 500 Worlds Titus booklet does not print these inline — they were provided by the Captain directly.

| Name | Points | Restriction |
|---|---|---|

| **Veteran of the Vanguard** | **20** | ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only |
| **Orbital Uplink Reliquary** | **25** | ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only |
| **Dedicated Gunship** | *TBD* | ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR model only |

### Laurels of Thunder — 15 pts

*This honour is awarded to those Space Marine officers who deploy greatest vigour during orbital drops.*

**ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only.** You can re-roll Charge rolls made for the bearer's unit in a turn in which it was set up on the battlefield.

### Veteran of the Vanguard — 20 pts

*Long experience leading Phobos-armoured vanguard strikes has taught this warrior many lessons about swift and stealthy attacks.*

**ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only.** Models in the bearer's unit have the **Scouts 6"** ability.

### Orbital Uplink Reliquary — 25 pts

*Though it appears a morbid trinket of faith, in truth this device contains a powerful microcogitator linked to the stratagem of the orbiting strike cruiser, which helps the bearer coordinate rapid strategic redeployments.*

**ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only.** After both players have deployed their armies, select up to **three ADEPTUS ASTARTES units** from your army and redeploy them. When doing so, you can set those units up in **Strategic Reserves** if you wish, **regardless of how many units are already in Strategic Reserves** (i.e. bypasses the 25% / 500pt Strike Force Strategic Reserves cap).

### Dedicated Gunship — TBD pts

*Bulky and somewhat lumbering as they are, Terminator squads — or even particularly vital officers wearing Terminator plate — may be assigned a gunship for swift combat extraction and relocation mid-battle.*

**ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR model only.** Once per battle, at the end of your opponent's Fight phase, if the bearer's unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, remove the bearer's unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves.

## Stratagems (1 CP each, 6 total)

### Suppression Strafing — 1 CP
*Orbital Assault Force — Strategic Ploy Stratagem*
- **WHEN:** Command phase.
- **TARGET:** One **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** unit from your army.
- **EFFECT:** Select one enemy unit visible to and within 18" of your unit. That enemy unit takes a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from that test. If failed, the unit is *suppressed* (−1 to Hit rolls) until your next Command phase.
- **RESTRICTIONS:** Cannot be used more than once per battle round.

### Tactical Decapitation — 1 CP
*Orbital Assault Force — Battle Tactic Stratagem*
- **WHEN:** Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.
- **TARGET:** One **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.
- **EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in your unit have the **[PRECISION]** ability, and each time a model in your unit makes an attack targeting a **CHARACTER** unit, add 1 to the Hit roll.

### Shock Onslaught — 1 CP
*Orbital Assault Force — Strategic Ploy Stratagem*
- **WHEN:** Fight phase.
- **TARGET:** One **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.
- **EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a **Pile-in or Consolidation** move, it can move up to **6"** instead of up to 3".

### Auto-sense Coordination — 1 CP
*Orbital Assault Force — Battle Tactic Stratagem*
- **WHEN:** Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.
- **TARGET:** One **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.
- **EFFECT:** Select the **[LETHAL HITS]** or **[SUSTAINED HITS 1]** ability. Until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in your unit have this ability in a turn in which they disembarked from a **DROP POD** or while targeting an enemy unit **within 12"**.

### Blind Screen — 1 CP
*Orbital Assault Force — Strategic Ploy Stratagem*
- **WHEN:** Your opponent's Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.
- **TARGET:** One **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** unit (excluding TITANIC) from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit's attacks, and one friendly **ADEPTUS ASTARTES VEHICLE** or **DROP POD** unit within 9" of it.
- **EFFECT:** Until the end of the phase, models in your units have the **Stealth** ability, and each time an attack targets one of those units, models in that unit have the **Benefit of Cover** against that attack.

### Onward for the Emperor — 1 CP
*Orbital Assault Force — Strategic Ploy Stratagem*
- **WHEN:** End of your opponent's Charge phase.
- **TARGET:** One **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** unit from your army that was not set up on the battlefield this turn, and one friendly **TRANSPORT** it is able to embark within.
- **EFFECT:** If your **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** unit is wholly within 9" of that **TRANSPORT**, it can embark within it.

## Composition / Restrictions

No specific unit exclusions listed. The detachment benefits heavily from DEEP STRIKE and DROP POD units but does not mandate them by rule.

**Note:** "Excluding TITANIC units" means Thunderhawks, Warlord Titans, etc. cannot be granted DS. All standard Space Marines units (including Vehicles like Repulsor Executioner, Impulsor, Redemptor, Ballistus) are eligible.
