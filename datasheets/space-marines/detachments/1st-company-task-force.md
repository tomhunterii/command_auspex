# 1st Company Task Force

**Faction:** Space Marines (Adeptus Astartes)
**Source:** Codex: Space Marines, 10th Edition / Wahapedia

The Veteran-focused detachment. Built around TERMINATOR and Veteran squads (Bladeguard, Sternguard, Vanguard) with re-roll Wounds against Oath target. No explicit composition minimums, but several Stratagems and 2 of 4 Enhancements are restricted to TERMINATOR models — take at least one to make the detachment worthwhile.

---

## Detachment Rule: Extremis-level Threat

Once per battle, in your Command phase, you can use this ability. If you do, until the start of your next Command phase, each time a model from your army with the Oath of Moment ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Wound roll as well.

## Stratagems

| Name                  | CP | Type           | Phase                       | Target                                                                          |
|-----------------------|----|----------------|-----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Armour of Contempt    | 1  | Battle Tactic  | Opponent's Shooting/Fight   | One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit (targeted)                                            |
| Heroes of the Chapter | 1  | Battle Tactic  | Your Shooting or Fight      | One TERMINATOR / BLADEGUARD / STERNGUARD / VANGUARD VETERAN unit (not yet acted)|
| Terrifying Proficiency| 1  | Strategic Ploy | Your Fight phase            | One eligible Veteran unit that charged & destroyed an enemy unit this phase     |
| Duty and Honour       | 1  | Strategic Ploy | Your Movement phase         | One eligible Veteran unit on an objective you control                           |
| Orbital Teleportarium | 1  | Strategic Ploy | End of opp. Fight phase     | One ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR unit                                            |
| Legendary Fortitude   | 1  | Battle Tactic  | Opp. Charge phase           | One eligible Veteran unit within Engagement Range of charging enemy             |

### Armour of Contempt

**Cost:** 1 CP | **Type:** Battle Tactic | **Phase:** Opponent's Shooting phase or Fight phase
**When:** Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.
**Target:** One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit's attacks.
**Effect:** Until the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, each time an attack targets your unit, worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.

### Heroes of the Chapter

**Cost:** 1 CP | **Type:** Battle Tactic | **Phase:** Your Shooting phase or Fight phase
**When:** Your Shooting phase or Fight phase.
**Target:** One ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR, BLADEGUARD VETERAN SQUAD, STERNGUARD VETERAN SQUAD or VANGUARD VETERAN SQUAD unit from your army that has not yet been selected to shoot or fight this phase.
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, add 1 to the Hit roll. If your unit is Below Half-strength, add 1 to the Wound roll of that attack as well.

### Terrifying Proficiency

**Cost:** 1 CP | **Type:** Strategic Ploy | **Phase:** Your Fight phase
**When:** Your Fight phase, after a unit from your army made a Charge move this turn and destroyed one or more enemy units this phase.
**Target:** That eligible unit.
**Effect:** In your opponent's next Command phase, each enemy unit within 6" of your unit must take a Battle-shock test. If the unit taking the test is Below Half-strength, subtract 1 from that test.

### Duty and Honour

**Cost:** 1 CP | **Type:** Strategic Ploy | **Phase:** Your Movement phase
**When:** Your Movement phase.
**Target:** One eligible Veteran unit (TERMINATOR / BLADEGUARD / STERNGUARD / VANGUARD VETERAN) from your army that is within range of an objective marker you control.
**Effect:** That objective marker remains under your control even if you have no models within range of it, until your opponent controls it at the start or end of any turn.

### Orbital Teleportarium

**Cost:** 1 CP | **Type:** Strategic Ploy | **Phase:** End of your opponent's Fight phase
**When:** End of your opponent's Fight phase.
**Target:** One ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR unit from your army that is not within Engagement Range of any enemy units.
**Effect:** Remove your unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves. It arrives back on the battlefield in the Reinforcements step of your next Movement phase using the Deep Strike ability.

### Legendary Fortitude

**Cost:** 1 CP | **Type:** Battle Tactic | **Phase:** Opponent's Charge phase
**When:** Your opponent's Charge phase, just after an enemy unit ends a Charge move.
**Target:** One eligible Veteran unit from your army that is within Engagement Range of that enemy unit.
**Effect:** Until the end of the turn, each time an attack is allocated to a model in your unit, subtract 1 from the Damage characteristic of that attack (to a minimum of 1).

## Enhancements

| Name                | Points | Restriction                           |
|---------------------|--------|---------------------------------------|
| The Imperium's Sword| 25     | ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only           |
| Fear Made Manifest  | 30     | ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only (Aura)    |
| Rites of War        | 10     | ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR model only|
| Iron Resolve        | 15     | ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR model only|

### The Imperium's Sword

**Points:** 25
**Restriction:** ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only.
**Effect:** Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by the bearer. Once per battle, at the start of any phase, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by all other models in the bearer's unit as well.

### Fear Made Manifest (Aura)

**Points:** 30
**Restriction:** ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only.
**Effect:** While an enemy unit (excluding MONSTERS and VEHICLES) is within 6" of the bearer, each time that unit fails a Battle-shock test, one model in that unit is destroyed. Once per battle, when such an enemy unit fails a Battle-shock test, you can choose for D3 models in that unit to be destroyed instead.

### Rites of War

**Points:** 10
**Restriction:** ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR model only.
**Effect:** Improve the Objective Control characteristic of the bearer by 1. Once per battle, at the start of any phase, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of all other models in the bearer's unit as well.

### Iron Resolve

**Points:** 15
**Restriction:** ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR model only.
**Effect:** The bearer has the Feel No Pain 5+ ability. Once per battle, after the bearer's unit is selected as the target of one or more attacks, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, until the end of that phase, models in the bearer's unit have the Feel No Pain 5+ ability.

---

## Composition Restrictions

None stated. However, 2 of 4 Enhancements (Rites of War, Iron Resolve) are TERMINATOR-only, and the Orbital Teleportarium Stratagem targets TERMINATORS only, so the detachment assumes at least one TERMINATOR squad.
