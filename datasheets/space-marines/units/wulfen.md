## Abilities

### Core
- None printed beyond faction/datasheet abilities.

### Faction
- **Oath of Moment** (Hit re-roll only — SW armies do not get the +1 to Wound).
- **Curse of the Wulfen** (army rule): WULFEN units have OC 0 by default, but while within 6" of a friendly SPACE WOLVES CHARACTER (excluding other WULFEN), or within 12" of a friendly WOLF PRIEST, and not Battle-shocked, their OC becomes 1 (INFANTRY) or 3 (VEHICLE).

### Savage Frenzy
Each time an enemy unit (excluding MONSTERS and VEHICLES) within Engagement Range of this unit Falls Back, all models in that enemy unit must take a Desperate Escape test. When doing so, if that enemy unit is Battle-shocked, subtract 1 from each of those tests.

### Death Totem (Wargear)
Each time the bearer makes a melee attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1.

## Led By

**This unit cannot be led by any Character.** The Wulfen datasheet has no LEADER entry on Wahapedia. Wolf Priest, Ragnar Blackmane, etc. do NOT list Wulfen in their "can lead" attachments.

> Exception: certain Enhancements (notably in the Saga of the Beastslayer detachment) allow a specific SPACE WOLVES CHARACTER to join a Wulfen unit. Confirm per-detachment before list-building.

---

## Advance + Charge?

**No.** The Wulfen datasheet does not carry a "eligible to declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced" rule. Blood Claws have Berserk Charge, but Wulfen do not. Wulfen rely on M9" + Savage Frenzy, not advance-charge.
