## Abilities

### Core
Leader

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Litany of Hate
While this model is leading a unit, each time a model in that unit makes a melee attack, add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Spiritual Leader
Once per battle, at the start of any phase, you can select one friendly Adeptus Astartes unit that is Battle-shocked and within 12" of this model. That unit is no longer Battle-shocked.

## Leader

This model can be attached to the following units:

- Assault Intercessor Squad
- Bladeguard Veteran Squad
- Hellblaster Squad
- Infernus Squad
- Intercessor Squad
- Sternguard Veteran Squad
- Tactical Squad

> **CANNOT lead Company Heroes** (notably absent; compare vs Captain).
> **CANNOT lead Victrix Honour Guard** (Captain-only unit).
> **CANNOT lead Gravis, Terminator, Inceptor, or Phobos units.** For those, use Chaplain in Terminator Armour (Terminators only) or Judiciar (Bladeguard/Sternguard) as appropriate.
