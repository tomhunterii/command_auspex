## Abilities

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Sternguard Focus
Each time a model in this unit makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Wound roll.

## Unit Composition

- 1 Sternguard Veteran Sergeant
- 4–9 Sternguard Veterans

Every model is equipped with: Sternguard bolt pistol; Sternguard bolt rifle; close combat weapon.

---

## Wargear Options

- The Sternguard Veteran Sergeant's Sternguard bolt rifle can be replaced with one of the following:
  - 1 Astartes chainsword
  - 1 combi-weapon
  - 1 power weapon
  - 1 power fist
- Any number of Sternguard Veterans can each have their Sternguard bolt rifle replaced with 1 combi-weapon.
- For every 5 models in this unit, 1 Sternguard Veteran's Sternguard bolt rifle can be replaced with one of the following: 1 Sternguard heavy bolter; 1 pyrecannon.
