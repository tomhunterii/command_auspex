## Abilities

### Core
None.

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Astartes Banner
While this unit contains an Ancient, add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in this unit.

### Command Squad
While a Character model is leading this unit, each time an attack targets this unit, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.

## Unit Composition

- 1 Ancient
- 1 Company Champion
- 2 Company Veterans

- The **Ancient** is equipped with: bolt pistol; bolt rifle; close combat weapon.
- The **Company Champion** is equipped with: bolt pistol; master-crafted power weapon.
- One **Company Veteran** is equipped with: bolt pistol; master-crafted bolt rifle; close combat weapon.
- One **Company Veteran** is equipped with: bolt pistol; master-crafted heavy bolter; close combat weapon.

**You must attach one Captain, Chapter Master, or Lieutenant model to this unit (this is not optional).**

## Leader

This unit is **not** a leader — it requires one to attach at army-build. The eligible leaders are exactly the datasheets named on Wahapedia's printed Led By list, not "any Captain/Lieutenant model."

**Wahapedia printed Led By list** (Codex: Space Marines, mono-SM): Captain, Captain Titus, Kor'sarro Khan, Lieutenant, Marneus Calgar in Armour of Antilochus, Pedro Kantor, Uriel Ventris, Vulkan He'stan, Adrax Agatone. (Blood Angels allies Captain Tycho, Ferren Areios, Gabriel Seth, and BA Captain also listed; not relevant for the Captain's mono-Ultramarines builds.)

**NOT on the list — cannot lead Company Heroes:**
- Captain in Gravis / Phobos / Terminator Armour, Captain with Jump Pack
- Chapter Master and its variant-armour datasheets (separate datasheets in 10th Ed)
- Lieutenant in Phobos / Reiver Armour, Primaris Lieutenant, Lieutenant with Combi-weapon
- Cato Sicarius (Victrix Honour Guard only)
- Chaplains, Librarians, Apothecaries, Techmarines, Ancients
