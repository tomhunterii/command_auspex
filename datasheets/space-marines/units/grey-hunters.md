## Abilities

### Core
- None printed beyond faction/datasheet abilities.

### Faction
- **Oath of Moment** (Hit re-roll only — SW armies do not get the +1 to Wound).
- **Curse of the Wulfen** (army rule) — does not buff this unit directly; applies to WULFEN keyword units only.

### Cunning Hunters
Each time a model in this unit makes an attack, re-roll a Wound roll of 1. If the target is within range of an objective marker, you can re-roll the Wound roll instead.

## Unit Composition

- 1 Grey Hunter Pack Leader
- 9 Grey Hunters

**Total: 10 models (fixed size).**

Every model is equipped with: bolt carbine; bolt pistol; Astartes chainsword.

## Wargear Options

- The Grey Hunter Pack Leader's bolt carbine can be replaced with 1 plasma pistol.
- The Grey Hunter Pack Leader's Astartes chainsword can be replaced with one of the following:
  - 1 power fist
  - 1 power weapon
