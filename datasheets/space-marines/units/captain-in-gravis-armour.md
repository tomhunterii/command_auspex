## Abilities

### Core
Leader

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Rites of Battle
Once per battle round, one unit from your army with this ability can use it when targeted with a Stratagem. If it does, reduce the CP cost of that Stratagem by 1CP, to a minimum of 0CP.

### Refuse to Yield
Each time an attack is allocated to this model, halve the Damage characteristic of that attack (rounding up).

## Leader

This model can be attached to the following units (all GRAVIS):

- Aggressor Squad
- Eradicator Squad
- Heavy Intercessor Squad

> **CANNOT lead Tacticus units** (Intercessor, Infernus, Hellblaster, Sternguard, Bladeguard, Tactical, Assault Intercessor). Tacticus leaders are the standard Captain.
> **CANNOT lead Terminator, Inceptor, Phobos units.**
> Indomitor Kill Team also appears on Wahapedia's list but is a Deathwatch unit — not legal in pure Ultramarines.
