## Abilities

### Core

- **Oath of Moment** (Hit re-roll only — SW armies do not get the +1 to Wound).
- **Curse of the Wulfen** (army rule) — does not grant a buff to this unit directly; applies to WULFEN keyword units only.

This unit is eligible to declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced.

- 1 Blood Claw Pack Leader
