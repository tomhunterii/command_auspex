# Wolf Guard Terminators

**Faction:** Space Marines (Adeptus Astartes) — Space Wolves Chapter
**Source:** Codex: Space Marines + Space Wolves Faction Pack (10th Edition) / Wahapedia

> Elite Space Wolves Terminators. Storm bolter + master-crafted power weapon baseline, with storm shield / assault cannon / power fist / relic greataxe / twin lightning claws options. Deep Strike, 2+/4++, Rugged Resilience against high-S attacks. Distinct from the generic Terminator Squad datasheet.

## Abilities

### Core
- **Deep Strike**

### Faction
- **Oath of Moment** (Hit re-roll only — SW armies do not get the +1 to Wound).
- **Curse of the Wulfen** (army rule) — does not buff this unit directly; applies to WULFEN keyword units only.

### Rugged Resilience
Each time an attack targets this unit, if the Strength characteristic of that attack is greater than the Toughness characteristic of this unit, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.

### Storm Shield (Wargear)
The bearer has a Wounds characteristic of 4.

> **Note:** The 4+ invulnerable save is the unit's base invulnerable (Terminator armour), not granted by the Storm Shield. The Storm Shield's rules benefit on this datasheet is +1 W (to 4) for the bearer, which stacks with the base 4++.

---

## Unit Composition

- 1 Wolf Guard Terminator Pack Leader
- 4–9 Wolf Guard Terminators

Every model is equipped with: storm bolter; master-crafted power weapon.

---

## Wargear Options

- Any number of models can each have their storm bolter replaced with 1 storm shield.
- For every 5 models in this unit, 1 Wolf Guard Terminator can have their storm bolter and master-crafted power weapon replaced with 1 assault cannon and 1 power fist.
- The Wolf Guard Terminator Pack Leader's master-crafted power weapon can be replaced with one of the following:
  - 1 relic greataxe
  - 1 twin lightning claws

> - **Wolf Priest in Terminator Armour:** NO, not on the list.
> - **Ulrik The Slayer:** NO, not on the list.
