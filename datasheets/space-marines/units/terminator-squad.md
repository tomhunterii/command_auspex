## Abilities

### Core
Deep Strike

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Teleport Homer
At the start of the battle, you can set up one Teleport Homer token for this unit anywhere on the battlefield that is not in your opponent's deployment zone. If you do, once per battle, you can target this unit with the Rapid Ingress Stratagem for 0CP, but when resolving that Stratagem, you must set this unit up within 3" of that token and not within 9" of any enemy models.

### Fury of the First
Each time a model in this unit makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, add 1 to the Hit roll.

## Unit Composition

- 1 Terminator Sergeant
- 4–9 Terminators

Every model is equipped with: storm bolter; power fist.

---

## Wargear Options

- The Terminator Sergeant's power fist can be replaced with 1 power weapon.
- Any number of Terminators can each have their power fist replaced with 1 chainfist.
- For every 5 models in this unit, 1 Terminator's storm bolter can be replaced with one of the following:
  - 1 assault cannon
  - 1 heavy flamer
  - 1 cyclone missile launcher (this Terminator retains its storm bolter)

## Led By

This unit can be led by the following Characters (TERMINATOR only):

- Ancient in Terminator Armour
- Captain in Terminator Armour
- Chaplain in Terminator Armour
- Librarian in Terminator Armour
- Marneus Calgar in Armour of Antilochus (confirmed via Terminator Squad Wahapedia entry)
- Darnath Lysander (Imperial Fists — not legal in pure Ultramarines)
- Belial (Dark Angels — not legal in pure Ultramarines)
- Deathwing Strikemaster (Dark Angels — not legal in pure Ultramarines)
- Wolf Guard Battle Leader in Terminator Armour (Space Wolves — not legal in pure Ultramarines)
- Caanok Var (World Eaters cross-listing — not legal)

> **CANNOT be led by standard Captain, Lieutenant, Chaplain, Librarian** — those are Tacticus-armour Characters. All Terminator Squad leaders must themselves be TERMINATOR.
