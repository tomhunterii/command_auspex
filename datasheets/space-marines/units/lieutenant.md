## Abilities

### Core
Leader

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Tactical Precision
While this model is leading a unit, weapons equipped by models in that unit have the [LETHAL HITS] ability.

### Target Priority
This model's unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Fell Back.

### Wargear Ability — Storm Shield
The bearer has a 4+ invulnerable save.

## Wargear Options

- This model's master-crafted bolter can be replaced with one of the following: 1 plasma pistol; 1 master-crafted power weapon; 1 power fist.
- Alternatively, this model's bolt pistol, master-crafted bolter and close combat weapon can be replaced with 1 neo-volkite pistol, 1 master-crafted power weapon and 1 storm shield.
- This model's bolt pistol can be replaced with 1 heavy bolt pistol.
- This model's close combat weapon can be replaced with 1 master-crafted power weapon or 1 power fist.

## Leader

This model can be attached to the following units:

- Assault Intercessor Squad
- Bladeguard Veteran Squad
- Company Heroes
- Hellblaster Squad
- Infernus Squad
- Intercessor Squad
- Sternguard Veteran Squad
- Tactical Squad

> **CONFIRMED via Wahapedia 2026-04-21: Lieutenant CANNOT lead Aggressor Squad, Eradicator Squad, or any GRAVIS unit.** Previous roster assumptions that a Lieutenant could buff Aggressors were INCORRECT — those units are GRAVIS-keyworded and require a GRAVIS leader (Captain in Gravis Armour, Apothecary Biologis, Marneus Calgar).
> **CANNOT lead Terminator, Inceptor, or Phobos units** either. Lieutenant is strictly a Tacticus/Battleline-infantry leader.
> **CANNOT lead Victrix Honour Guard** (Captain-only unit).
