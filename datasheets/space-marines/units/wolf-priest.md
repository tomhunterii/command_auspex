## Abilities

### Core
- Leader

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

> **IMPORTANT:** A Space Wolves army has the Space Wolves keyword, so the Wolf Priest (and his led unit) does NOT receive the +1 to Wound — only the Hit re-roll.

### Healing Balms
While this model is leading a unit, in your Command phase, you can return 1 destroyed model (excluding CHARACTER models) to that unit.

### Litany of Hate
While this model is leading a unit, each time a model in that unit makes a melee attack, add 1 to the Wound roll.

> Wahapedia does NOT list a named ability called "Fervent Acclamation" on this datasheet. The unit-specific abilities are **Healing Balms** and **Litany of Hate**.

## Leader

This Character can attach to the following units:

- **Blood Claws**
- **Grey Hunters**
- **Wolf Guard Headtakers**

> Wahapedia's Leader list is exactly these three units. The Wolf Priest **CANNOT** attach to: Wolf Guard Terminators, Intercessor Squad, Assault Intercessor Squad, Infernus Squad, Hellblaster Squad, Inceptor Squad, Bladeguard Veterans, Sternguard Veterans, Reivers, or any other unit. This is load-bearing: a Logan Grimnar + Wolf Guard Terminators bomb **cannot** also carry a Wolf Priest.
