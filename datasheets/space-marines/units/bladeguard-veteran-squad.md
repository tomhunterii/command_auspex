## Abilities

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Bladeguard
At the start of the Fight phase, you can select one of the following abilities to apply to models in this unit until the end of the phase:
- **Swords of the Chapter:** Each time a model in this unit makes a melee attack, re-roll a Hit roll of 1.
- **Shields of the Chapter:** Each time an invulnerable saving throw is made for a model in this unit, re-roll a saving throw of 1.

## Unit Composition

- 1 Bladeguard Veteran Sergeant
- 2–5 Bladeguard Veterans

Every model is equipped with: heavy bolt pistol; master-crafted power weapon.

## Led By

This unit can be led by the following Characters (mono-Codex SM / Ultramarines):

- Adrax Agatone
- Bladeguard Ancient
- Captain (requires relic shield to attach)
- Captain Titus
- Chaplain
- Chief Librarian Tigurius
- Judiciar
- Kor'sarro Khan
- Lieutenant
- Marneus Calgar in Armour of Antilochus
- Pedro Kantor
- Uriel Ventris
- Primaris Company Champion

> **Attachment restriction on Captain:** standard (non-Titus) Captain can only attach to Bladeguard Veteran Squad if equipped with a relic shield. See datasheet notes in `captain.md`.
> Wahapedia list also includes faction-specific leaders (Chaplain Kastiel, Judiciar Xacharus, Sanguinary Priest, Asmodai/Azrael/Ezekiel/Lazarus, Captain Tycho, Ferren Areios, Gabriel Seth). Those are NOT legal in pure Ultramarines lists.
