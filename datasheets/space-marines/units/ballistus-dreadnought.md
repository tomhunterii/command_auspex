## Abilities

### Core
**Deadly Demise D3.** When this model is destroyed, roll one D6: on a 6, units within 6" suffer D3 mortal wounds.

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** Standard — re-roll Hit rolls vs Oath target; add 1 to Wound rolls vs that target in mono-SM Codex lists.

### Ballistus Strike
Each time this model makes a ranged attack targeting a unit that is **not Below Half-strength**, you can re-roll the Hit roll.

### Damaged: 1-4 Wounds Remaining
While this model has 1-4 wounds remaining, each time it makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.
