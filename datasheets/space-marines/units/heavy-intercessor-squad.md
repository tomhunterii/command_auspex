## Abilities

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Unyielding in the Face of the Foe
While a model in this unit is within range of an objective marker you control, each time an attack with a Damage characteristic of 1 is allocated to that model, add 1 to any armour saving throw made against that attack.

## Unit Composition

- 1 Heavy Intercessor Sergeant

---

## Wargear Options

- For every 5 models in this unit, 1 Heavy Intercessor's heavy bolt rifle can be replaced with 1 heavy bolter.

> **Conflict flag (per MFM-skepticism directive):** Captain Hunter's brief anticipated kit-side options for executor / hellstorm bolt rifle, hellfire bolt rifle, sergeant heavy bolt pistol + power weapon swaps. Those options exist on the **plastic kit** and were on the **9th Edition** datasheet, but **10th Edition Codex: Space Marines simplified the datasheet** — the only legal swap is heavy bolt rifle → heavy bolter, 1 per 5 models. Wahapedia, 40k.app, and newrecruit.eu all confirm. Adopted Wahapedia. Captain's roster Squad IV ("Sgt + 4 with heavy bolt rifles") is fully legal under 10th wargear; "executor bolt rifle" naming in the roster is cosmetic / kit-flavour only.

## Led By

This unit can be led by the following Characters (mono-Codex SM / Ultramarines):

- Apothecary Biologis
- Captain in Gravis Armour
- Marneus Calgar in Armour of Antilochus

> Wahapedia's full Led By list also includes **Iron Father Feirros** (Iron Hands Chapter character), **Tor Garadon** (Imperial Fists), and seven Inquisitor variants under Imperial Agents allies (Inquisitor, Coteaz, Draxus, Greyfax, Eisenhorn, Inquisitor in Terminator Armour, Inquisitor Ostromandeus). All are rules-legal in mono-Codex SM lists; none are excluded for legality. They are absent from `led_by:` only because no datasheets for those slugs exist in this repo. Iron Father Feirros and Tor Garadon are flavour-incongruous with Ultramarines but rules-legal — note this if a future engagement borrows one as an ally.
> **CANNOT** be led by standard Captain, standard Lieutenant, Captain in Phobos Armour, Captain in Terminator Armour, Captain Demetrian Titus, Lieutenant in Phobos Armour, Lieutenant in Reiver Armour, Chaplain, generic Apothecary, Judiciar, or any Tacticus / Phobos / Terminator-only character. Gravis bodyguards require Gravis-keyworded leaders (or Marneus Calgar via "Master of the Chapter").
> Not an Epic Hero — squad CAN host an Enhancement on its leader (subject to that leader's own eligibility).
