## Abilities

### Core
Leader

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** Standard Adeptus Astartes rule — re-roll Hit rolls (and add 1 to Wound rolls in mono-Codex SM lists) against the designated Oath target.

### Surgical Precision
While this model is leading a unit, weapons equipped by models in that unit have the **[LETHAL HITS]** ability.

### Vivispectrum
If this model's unit destroys an enemy unit as the result of a melee attack, until the end of the battle, this model has an Objective Control characteristic of **9**.

> **NOTE:** This datasheet has **no** Feel No Pain ability, **no** healing/revive, and **no** narthecium-style "restore a destroyed model" function. Those are abilities of the Standard *Apothecary* (Tacticus, Phobos variants), NOT the Apothecary Biologis. Biologis is an OFFENSIVE buff character, not a defensive support.
