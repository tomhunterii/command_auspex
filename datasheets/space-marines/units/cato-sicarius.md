## Abilities

### Core
Leader

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Knight Champion of Macragge
Once per turn, when an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move within 9" of this model's unit, if this unit is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, it can make a Normal move of up to 6".

### Honour or Death
You can target this unit with the Heroic Intervention Stratagem for 0CP, even if you have already used that Stratagem on a different unit this phase.

### Suzerain of Talassar (army restriction)
Cato Sicarius can be attached to a Victrix Honour Guard even if Marneus Calgar is already attached to that unit.
