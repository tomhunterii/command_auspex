## Abilities

### Core
Leader (cannot be attached — see Supreme Commander)

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Author of the Codex
At the start of your Command phase, select two Author of the Codex abilities. Until the start of your next Command phase, this model has those abilities. Options:
- **Primarch of the XIII (Aura):** While a friendly Adeptus Astartes unit is within 6" of this model, models in that unit have +1 OC and can re-roll Battle-shock and Leadership tests.
- **Master of Battle:** At the start of your Command phase, you can select one additional enemy unit to be a secondary Oath of Moment target. If the primary Oath of Moment target is destroyed, the secondary becomes the primary.
- **Supreme Strategist:** Once per battle round, you can reduce the Command Point cost of a Stratagem used to affect a friendly Adeptus Astartes unit within 12" of this model by 1CP, to a minimum of 0CP.

### Ultramarines Bodyguard
While this model is within 3" of one or more friendly Adeptus Astartes Infantry units, this model has the Lone Operative ability.

### Armour of Fate
The first time this model is destroyed, roll one D6 at the end of the phase: on a 3+, set this model back up on the battlefield, as close as possible to where it was destroyed and not within Engagement Range of any enemy models, with 6 wounds remaining.

### Supreme Commander (army restriction)
If your army includes Roboute Guilliman, he must be your Warlord, and Roboute Guilliman cannot be attached to a Bodyguard unit.
