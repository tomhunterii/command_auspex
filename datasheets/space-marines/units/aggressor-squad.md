## Abilities

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Close-quarters Firepower
Each time a model in this unit makes a ranged attack that targets the closest eligible target, improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.

## Unit Composition

- 1 Aggressor Sergeant
- 2–5 Aggressors

Every model is equipped with: flamestorm gauntlets; twin power fists.

---

## Wargear Options

- All models in this unit can each have their flamestorm gauntlets replaced with 1 auto boltstorm gauntlets and 1 fragstorm grenade launcher.

> **Note:** The swap is per-model (not unit-wide), so squads CAN be mixed — but in practice players run the unit as a single loadout. The two practical configurations are:
>
> | Loadout | Per-Model Wargear | Role |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Flamestorm** (default) | flamestorm gauntlets + twin power fists | Anti-horde overwatch torrent |
> | **Boltstorm + Fragstorm** (common alternate) | auto boltstorm gauntlets + fragstorm grenade launcher + twin power fists | Mid-range AP-piercing fire + blast |
>
> **Captain Hunter's Squad XII "Ixion" runs the Boltstorm + Fragstorm loadout** (per `ultramarines/2nd-company.md`).

## Led By

This unit can be led by the following Characters (only):

- **Apothecary Biologis**
- **Captain in Gravis Armour**
- **Iron Father Feirros** (Iron Hands — not legal in pure Ultramarines lists)
- **Marneus Calgar in Armour of Antilochus**
- **Tor Garadon** (Imperial Fists — not legal in pure Ultramarines lists)

> **For pure Ultramarines (Blade of Ultramar) lists, the legal Aggressor leaders are: Apothecary Biologis, Captain in Gravis Armour, or Marneus Calgar.** Standard Lieutenant, Captain (on foot, Tacticus), Chaplain (Tacticus), and Captain in Terminator Armour CANNOT lead Aggressors.
