## Abilities

### Core
- Deadly Demise 1
- Feel No Pain 5+
- Smoke (from keyword)

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

> **IMPORTANT:** A Space Wolves army has the Space Wolves keyword, so Bjorn does NOT receive the +1 to Wound bonus — only Hit re-roll.

### Legendary Tenacity
Each time an attack targets this model, if the Strength characteristic of that attack is greater than this model's Toughness characteristic, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.

### Ancient Tactician
At the start of your Command phase, if this model is on the battlefield, you gain 1CP.

> Wahapedia does NOT list a distinct "Fell-Handed" named ability; his signature rules on the datasheet are **Legendary Tenacity** and **Ancient Tactician**. No separate aura/buff to nearby units is printed.
