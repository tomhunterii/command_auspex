## Abilities

### Core
Scouts 6"

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Fearsome Assault
At the start of the Fight phase, each enemy unit within Engagement Range of one or more units with this ability must take a Battle-shock test, subtracting 1 from that test.

### Terror Troops (Aura)
While an enemy unit (excluding MONSTERS and VEHICLES) is within 3" of one or more units with this ability, subtract 1 from the Objective Control characteristic of models in that enemy unit.

### Grapnel Launcher (Wargear)
Each time the bearer's unit makes a Normal, Advance, Fall Back or Charge move, ignore any vertical distance when determining the total distance the bearer can be moved during that move.

### Reiver Grav-chute (Wargear)
The bearer has the Deep Strike ability.

## Unit Composition

- 1 Reiver Sergeant
- 4–9 Reivers

Every model is equipped with: special issue bolt pistol; combat knife.

---

## Wargear Options

- All models can each have their combat knife replaced with 1 bolt carbine and 1 close combat weapon.
- The Reiver Sergeant, if equipped with a bolt carbine, can additionally be equipped with a combat knife.
- All models can each be equipped with 1 Reiver grav-chute (gains Deep Strike).
- All models can each be equipped with 1 grapnel launcher.

---

## Led By

This unit can be led by the following Characters (PHOBOS only):

- Captain in Phobos Armour
- Librarian in Phobos Armour
- Lieutenant in Phobos Armour
- Lieutenant in Reiver Armour

> **CANNOT be led by standard Captain / Lieutenant / Chaplain / Librarian** — those are Tacticus; Reivers are PHOBOS.
