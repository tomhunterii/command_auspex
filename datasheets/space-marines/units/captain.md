## Abilities

### Core
Leader

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Rites of Battle
Once per battle round, one unit from your army with this ability can use it when targeted with a Stratagem. If it does, reduce the CP cost of that Stratagem by 1CP, to a minimum of 0CP.

### Finest Hour (Once per battle)
Once per battle, at the start of the Fight phase, this model can use this ability. If it does, until the end of the phase, add 3 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons equipped by this model and those weapons have the [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] ability.

### Wargear Ability — Relic Shield
The bearer has a Wounds characteristic of 6.

## Leader

This model can be attached to the following units:

- Assault Intercessor Squad
- Bladeguard Veteran Squad *(requires relic shield)*
- Company Heroes
- Hellblaster Squad *(requires plasma pistol)*
- Infernus Squad
- Intercessor Squad
- Sternguard Veteran Squad
- Tactical Squad
- Victrix Honour Guard

> **Wargear-conditional attachment restrictions (Wahapedia, verbatim note):** "This model cannot be attached to a BLADEGUARD VETERAN SQUAD unless this model is equipped with a relic shield, and cannot be attached to a HELLBLASTER SQUAD unless this model is equipped with a plasma pistol."
> **CANNOT lead Gravis units** (Aggressor, Eradicator, Heavy Intercessor) — use Captain in Gravis Armour instead.
> **CANNOT lead Terminator units** — use Captain in Terminator Armour instead.
> **CANNOT lead Inceptors, Outriders, or Phobos units.**
