## Abilities

### Core
Feel No Pain 5+, Leader

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

### Press the Attack
Weapons equipped by models in this model's unit have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability.

### Honour of Ultramar
If this model is destroyed by a melee attack, if it has not fought this phase, roll one D6: on a 2+, do not remove it from play. This model can fight after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks. If one or more enemy models are destroyed as a result of those attacks, this model regains D3 lost wounds and is not destroyed; otherwise, it is removed from play.

### Demetrian Titus (army restriction)
Your army cannot include more than one Titus unit.

## Leader

This model can be attached to the following units:

- Assault Intercessor Squad
- Bladeguard Veteran Squad
- Company Heroes
- Hellblaster Squad
- Infernus Squad
- Intercessor Squad
- Sternguard Veteran Squad
- Victrix Honour Guard
- Wardens of Ultramar

> **Titus exception:** Unlike the standard Captain, Titus does NOT require a relic shield to lead Bladeguard nor a plasma pistol to lead Hellblasters. His wargear is fixed (master-crafted bolter + master-crafted chainsword) and his LEADER list allows these attachments unconditionally.
> **CANNOT lead Tactical Squad** — notably absent from his list, despite being on the standard Captain's. The Wardens of Ultramar entry is Titus-only (it's a named Ultramarines unit).
> **CANNOT lead Gravis, Terminator, Inceptor, or Phobos units.**
