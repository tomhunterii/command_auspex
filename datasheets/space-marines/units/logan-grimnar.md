## Abilities

### Core
- Deep Strike
- Leader

### Faction
**Oath of Moment.** If your Army Faction is Adeptus Astartes, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your Oath of Moment target. Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll, and if you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch or Space Wolves keywords, you can also add 1 to the Wound roll.

> **IMPORTANT:** A Space Wolves army DOES include Space Wolves keyword units, therefore Logan and his army DO NOT receive the +1 to Wound bonus — only the Hit re-roll.

### High King of Fenris
Once per battle round, in your Movement phase, you can select one friendly SPACE WOLVES unit that is in Reserves. If you do, until the end of the phase, for the purpose of setting up that unit on the battlefield, treat the current battle round number as being one higher than it actually is.

### Guile of the Wolf (Aura)
Each time your opponent targets a unit from their army with a Stratagem, if that unit is within 12" of this model, increase the cost of that usage of that Stratagem by 1CP (this is not cumulative with any other rules that increase the CP cost of that Stratagem).
