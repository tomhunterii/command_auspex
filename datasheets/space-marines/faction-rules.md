# Space Marines — Faction Rules

## Oath of Moment

If your Army Faction is **ADEPTUS ASTARTES**, at the start of your Command phase, select one unit from your opponent's army. Until the start of your next Command phase, that enemy unit is your **Oath of Moment target**.

Each time a model with this ability makes an attack that targets your Oath of Moment target, you can re-roll the Hit roll. If you are using a Codex: Space Marines Detachment and your army does not include one or more units with the BLACK TEMPLARS, BLOOD ANGELS, DARK ANGELS, DEATHWATCH or SPACE WOLVES keywords, add 1 to the Wound roll as well.
