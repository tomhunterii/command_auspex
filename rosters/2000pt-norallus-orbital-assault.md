## Key Stratagem Economy

Start of each turn budget check. Stratagems to reserve CP for:

| Stratagem | Cost | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Tactical Decapitation** | 1 CP | Precision + +1 Hit vs CHARACTER — snipe Lord Exultant, Lucius |
| **Auto-sense Coordination** | 1 CP | Lethal Hits / Sustained Hits 1 within 12" — buff Aggressors or Eradicators |
| **Shock Onslaught** | 1 CP | 6" Pile-in/Consolidation — reposition for Breakthrough |
| **Armour of Contempt** | 1 CP | −1 AP incoming — protect Sternguard deathstar from EC AP fire |
| **Suppression Strafing** | 1 CP | Battle-shock test on enemy within 18" — once per battle round |
| **Blind Screen** | 1 CP | Stealth + Cover for unit + friendly VEHICLE within 9" |

Expect ~6-8 CP per game. Spend freely; hoarding CP doesn't win games.
